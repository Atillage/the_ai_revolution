Disclosure and privacy

How this site earns money, what it collects and what it doesn't. Last updated November 2026.

Paid links

Some links on this site are paid (affiliate) links. They're marked "Paid link" next to the link. If you click one and sign up or buy, The AI Revolution may earn a commission. It doesn't change the price you pay.

A commission never decides whether something is listed. Each partner section also includes free or low-cost options, and some services are listed without any paid link at all.

My own products

The guides sold on Gumroad are my own products. Buying one supports the site directly.

Newsletter signups

Email forms on this site send you to Substack to finish subscribing to AI Revolution Update, my free newsletter about AI and everyday life. Your email is handled by Substack under its privacy policy. This site doesn't store your email address.

Calculators and quizzes

The AI Readiness Score, the calculators, the learning path finder and the healthcare task check run entirely in your browser. You don't need to give an email or sign up to use them, there's nothing to buy at the end, and your answers aren't sent to or saved by this site.

Visitor statistics

This site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites. Affiliate networks may also count clicks on paid links, and the sites you visit through any outside link use their own cookies and privacy policies.

Accounts and payments

This site has no accounts, no comments and no shopping cart. Purchases happen on the seller's own site.

General information only

Content on this site is general educational information, not medical, financial, legal or career advice. Programs, prices and offers change, so confirm details with the provider before you sign up or buy.
