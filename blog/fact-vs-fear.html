AI: fact versus fear

Every major technological change brings excitement and concern. Artificial intelligence deserves thoughtful questions, not blind trust or constant fear.

Fact: tools have limitations

AI can generate convincing but incorrect information. It can also reproduce bias and reveal risks when people share confidential data.

Fear: every prediction is inevitable

Forecasts about the future of work are scenarios, not guarantees.

Our approach

Try tools, check claims against reliable sources and stay open to learning.
