Is AI replacing jobs?

AI is changing how some tasks get done. It is less helpful to focus on sweeping predictions than to ask which tasks are changing, which skills remain valuable and where new opportunities appear.

Jobs are bundles of tasks

AI may change some tasks within a job without automatically replacing every worker who performs that job.

Skills worth practicing

Clear communication, subject expertise, verification, collaboration and willingness to learn new systems remain useful.

What to watch

Look for credible research, real employer adoption and concrete examples—not sensational headlines alone.
