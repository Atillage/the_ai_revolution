AI is here. What does that mean for us?

AI is no longer an idea from science fiction. It is appearing in everyday products and at work. The useful question is not whether we should panic, but where it can help—and when we should be cautious.

Start with curiosity

You do not need to master the technology overnight. Try one simple use case, such as brainstorming a meal plan or organizing a project.

Keep your judgment

AI-generated responses can be incomplete or wrong. Double-check important facts, especially decisions about health, money and work.

One small experiment

Think of one repetitive task in your day and ask whether an AI tool could make it easier. Evaluate the result yourself.
