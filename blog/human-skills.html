What human skills matter in an AI world?

The future of work is not only about learning the newest software. It is also about understanding how to combine useful tools with distinctly human strengths.

Critical thinking

Ask good questions, recognize missing context and independently verify important outputs.

Empathy and trust

Working well with people requires listening, ethical judgment and an awareness of their needs.

Adaptability

Learning one useful skill at a time is often more sustainable than trying to master everything at once.
