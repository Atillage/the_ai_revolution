Search smarter

  • Add "no degree" or "or equivalent experience" to any job search to find jobs that hire for skills.
  • Set up job alerts and apply in the first few days a job is posted.
  • Watch for scams. A real employer will never ask you to pay to apply, buy equipment up front or deposit a check for them.

Get hired faster with AI

Five prompts for your job search

Paste one into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and fill in the brackets. Always read the result and make it sound like you. Never paste your Social Security number or bank details.

Match my resume to this job

Here is my resume: [paste]. Here is the job post: [paste]. Rewrite my summary and bullet points to match this job, using only experience I really have. Then list any skills from the post that I'm missing.

Explain a layoff or a gap

I was laid off from my job as a [job] in [month] because [reason, like company cutbacks]. Write two calm, positive sentences I can say in an interview about it, then one sentence that turns to what I can do for them.

Practice an interview

Act as a hiring manager for a [job title] job. Ask me one interview question at a time. After each answer, tell me what was strong and how to make it better.

Find jobs my skills fit

I worked as a [job] for [years] years. List the skills I built that other employers want, in the words they use, and suggest 5 job titles I could apply for without a four-year degree.

Write a thank-you email

Write a short thank-you email after my interview for the [job] position at [company] with [name]. Mention [something we talked about]. Keep it under 120 words.

Many employers use software to screen resumes. Jobscan shows how yours scores against a job post (free scans, paid plan for more).
