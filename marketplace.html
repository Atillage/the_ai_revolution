No Wi-Fi smart home

One click, every light

You don't need an app, a hub or Wi-Fi for a smart home. Bulbs and battery remotes can talk to each other directly.

01

Pick one system

IKEA, Philips Hue or Lutron Caséta. Stay with one brand to start.

02

Pair the remote

Follow the pairing steps in the box. It usually takes a minute per bulb or switch.

03

Put it where you need it

Battery remotes mount anywhere: by the bed, the couch or the door.

04

Add an app later, if ever

A hub adds schedules and away-from-home control. It's optional.

Before you buy

Five questions to ask about any AI gadget

  • Does it have a camera or microphone? Know who can see and hear what it records, and where that goes.
  • Is there a subscription? Many AI gadgets need a monthly plan for the smart features.
  • Is it first-generation? First versions are exciting and rough. Waiting a year often means better and cheaper.
  • Does it work with what you have? Look for the Matter logo if you want it to work across Apple, Google, Amazon and Samsung.
  • What's the return window? Buy from a store with an easy return policy, and test it in the first week.

Recording people? Always ask first. Some states require everyone's consent.
