FIRST GUIDE

Start using AI at work in three steps

01

Pick a repetitive task

Choose something low-risk, such as organizing a to-do list, outlining a meeting agenda, or drafting a generic email.

02

Try a small AI prompt

Describe your task, desired format, and audience. Start with non-confidential information and review every result.

03

Build a reliable workflow

Save a useful prompt, check accuracy, and use AI only where your workplace policies permit it.
