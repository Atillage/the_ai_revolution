FIRST GUIDE

Make your home smarter in three steps

01

Choose a system

Start with Apple Home, Google Home, or Amazon Alexa. Consider what phones and devices your household already uses.

02

Pick one simple task

Try switching on a lamp at sunset or setting a voice-controlled morning routine. You don't need a full smart home on day one.

03

Check compatibility

Before buying, confirm support for your chosen system, Wi-Fi requirements, any hub you may need, and ongoing subscription fees.
