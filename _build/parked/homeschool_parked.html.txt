<div class="center-head"><span class="eyebrow">The options, in plain words</span><h2>Four ways kids are learning now</h2></div>
<div class="path-grid">
<article class="topic-card solid"><h3>Online school</h3><p>A full school delivered online, often free through a public virtual school in your state. It has teachers, a schedule and grades, just at home.</p></article>
<article class="topic-card solid"><h3>Homeschooling</h3><p>Parents lead the learning, usually with a curriculum, a co-op or outside classes. Most homeschool families use some outside help.</p></article>
<article class="topic-card solid"><h3>Deschooling</h3><p>A break after leaving traditional school, so a child can recover from school routines and rediscover what they're curious about before starting something new.</p></article>
<article class="topic-card solid"><h3>Unschooling</h3><p>Child-led learning through interests, projects and everyday life, without a set curriculum. The parent guides and provides resources.</p></article>
