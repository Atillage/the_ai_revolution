Helping working people stay ahead.

The AI Revolution helps working people stay ahead of the AI economy by changing how they earn, learn, live and build wealth.

Why this site exists

AI is changing who gets hired, how rent is set, how money is managed, how kids learn and how health care gets delivered. Most of the coverage is written for investors and tech workers. This site is written for everyone else: people who work for a paycheck and want a plan.

Who's behind it

I'm Alaina. I've spent more than 30 years working in clinical health care, and I've watched technology change that work from the inside. I started The AI Revolution to share what I'm learning in simple terms, and to point people toward real help instead of hype.

The five pillars

  • Work: keep earning when AI can do parts of your job.
  • Housing: know your rights when AI helps decide rent and approvals, and build a safety net.
  • Finance: build security that doesn't depend on one paycheck.
  • Education: find the learning path that fits your family.
  • Healthcare Work: see which tasks are changing and how to stay ahead.

Adapt. Build. Evolve.

Adapt by understanding what's changing. Build skills, savings and options. Evolve by finding what works for your own life.
