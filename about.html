ABOUT THE PROJECT

Learning together in a changing world.

The AI Revolution exists to help everyday people navigate the age of artificial intelligence.

We don't need to have all the answers.

AI is changing work, creativity, education and ordinary routines. For many people, that is exciting—and confusing. This project starts with curiosity, clear explanations and useful experiments.

Adapt. Build. Evolve.

Adapt by asking questions. Build by trying tools and learning new skills. Evolve by finding what works for your own life.
